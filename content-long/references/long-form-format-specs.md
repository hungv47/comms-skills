# Long-Form Format Specs

Per-format specifications for content-long: word count ranges, structure expectations, schema, distribution requirements, compliance.

---

## Pillar Guide

**Purpose:** Authoritative, evergreen guide that ranks for a head-term informational query, anchors a topic cluster, and earns earned media + internal-link equity.

**Word count:** 2500-5000 words (top-5 SERP median ± 20% from brief)

**Structure:**
1. **TL;DR** (40-80 words) — featured-snippet candidate
2. **Lede + nut graf** (60-100 words)
3. **H2 sections** (4-8) — each with sub-H3s where outline calls
4. **Counter-argument H2** (if brief has one)
5. **FAQ section** (with FAQPage schema, PAA-verbatim wording)
6. **Synthesis / next steps** + CTA

**Schema:** Article + FAQPage + HowTo (if procedural section) + BreadcrumbList

**Distribution:**
- Internal-link map (FROM existing pages, TO future cluster pieces)
- Newsletter feature
- LinkedIn share queue
- Authority outreach (cite-and-share to authorities cited in body)
- Repurpose: derived social posts, derived video script (cascade to content-short)

**Compliance:** None special

**Voice:** Authoritative, structured, scannable. Reading level grade 9-12.

## Standard Blog Post

**Purpose:** Mid-depth post that ranks for a long-tail or specific intent query, supports a topic cluster.

**Word count:** 1200-2500 words

**Structure:**
1. Lede + nut graf (60-100 words)
2. H2 sections (3-6)
3. Counter-argument H2 (if applicable)
4. Conclusion + CTA

**Schema:** Article + BreadcrumbList; FAQPage if FAQ section

**Distribution:**
- Internal links to related pillar
- Cluster-piece status if part of a cluster
- Single newsletter mention typical

**Compliance:** None special

**Voice:** Pillar's voice, possibly slightly less formal

## Case Study

**Purpose:** Demonstrate proof through a specific protagonist's before/after with named metrics.

**Word count:** 1500-3000 words

**Structure:**
1. **TL;DR** (the result in one paragraph — "How [Protagonist] [outcome] in [timeframe]")
2. **Protagonist + starting situation** — with specific metrics, role, scale
3. **The challenge / decision** — what triggered them to act
4. **Approach** — what they did, in detail
5. **Outcome** — with metrics (vs. starting state)
6. **Lessons learned** — generalizable insights
7. **How others can apply** — adoption path
8. **CTA** — related case studies + demo/talk-to-sales

**Schema:** Article + Review (or CaseStudy structured data) + Organization (for protagonist)

**Distribution:**
- Customer-stories page placement
- Sales enablement (deck, one-pager)
- Co-marketing with protagonist (their newsletter, their LinkedIn share)
- Demo/sales CTAs

**Compliance:**
- **Written consent from protagonist** (case study release form)
- **Logo usage rights**
- **Metrics double-checked with protagonist**
- **Quote approvals from named individuals**

**Voice:** Narrative, specific, metrics-anchored. Reads like a story with data.

## Byline Article

**Purpose:** Earn third-party authority by publishing under our name in a trusted outlet (TechCrunch, TheNewStack, InfoQ, Pragmatic Engineer, etc.).

**Word count:** Outlet-specific (typically 800-2000 words)

**Structure:**
1. **Hook lede** — outlet style (story, contrarian, stat — varies by outlet)
2. **Thesis paragraph** — what this piece argues
3. **3-5 main argument sections** (with H2 if outlet supports)
4. **Counter-argument** (mandatory in byline format — earns credibility)
5. **Conclusion** — implications, call to thought
6. **Author bio** — outlet-specific format

**Schema:** Article + Author (typically NOT our schema — outlet's CMS handles)

**Distribution:**
- Outlet handles primary publication
- Cross-post on our domain after exclusivity period (typically 30-90 days, outlet-specific)
- LinkedIn share with author quote
- Newsletter mention
- Authority outreach (the author becomes the authority)

**Compliance:**
- **Outlet style guide** — review and follow before submission
- **Outlet exclusivity** — typically 30-90 days before cross-post
- **Author bio** + **bio photo** per outlet spec
- **No competitor mentions** unless outlet allows
- **Pitch process** — most outlets require pitch before draft

**Voice:** Outlet's voice + our author's voice. Less promotional than our own blog. Authority-first.

## Press Release

**Purpose:** Announce news (funding, product launch, milestone, hire, partnership) to press for earned media coverage.

**Word count:** 400-800 words

**Structure:**
1. **Embargo line** (if applicable) — "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE" or "EMBARGOED UNTIL [date/time/timezone]"
2. **Headline** — clear, news-shaped, ≤80 chars
3. **Subhead** (optional) — adds context
4. **Dateline** — CITY, STATE — DATE
5. **Lede paragraph** — 5W1H: who, what, when, where, why, how
6. **Quote from spokesperson** — typically CEO/founder/relevant exec
7. **Body paragraphs** (2-4) — supporting detail, context, secondary quote
8. **Boilerplate** — "About [Company]" — standard ≈100 words
9. **Contact info** — press contact name, email, phone
10. **End marker** — ### or -30-

**Schema:** NewsArticle + Organization

**Distribution:**
- Wire service (PR Newswire, Business Wire, GlobeNewswire) for broad reach
- Direct outlet pitches to journalists from Source Map
- Embargo coordination with key journalists (offer exclusive in exchange for early coverage)
- Media kit (logos, exec photos, fact sheet, additional quotes)
- Internal: investor update, all-hands announcement
- Social: founder LinkedIn, company social

**Compliance:**
- **Embargo metadata** — clearly marked
- **Forward-looking statement disclaimers** if public company (safe-harbor language)
- **Spokesperson approved quotes**
- **Legal review** for any claims about competitors, acquisitions, regulated topics
- **Contact info accurate** — press will contact this number

**Voice:** Third-person, news-style, AP style. Confident but factual. NO marketing language.

## Newsletter Feature (Own)

**Purpose:** Weekly/biweekly long-form for our own newsletter audience — drives engagement, retention, share-rate.

**Word count:** 800-2000 words (varies by newsletter cadence and audience)

**Structure:**
1. **Personal/conversational hook** (1-2 sentences)
2. **Setup / context**
3. **Body** — voice-driven, less SEO-formal
4. **Pull-quote** — share-worthy line, often called out visually
5. **CTA** — reply, share, or specific ask

**Schema:** Article (if also published on our domain) — newsletter platforms typically don't carry our schema

**Distribution:**
- Newsletter primary
- Cross-post to LinkedIn (often as long-form post)
- Substack / Beehiiv note for snippet tease
- Optional: Twitter/X thread of key points
- Repurpose to derived blog post (after 1-2 weeks of newsletter exclusivity)

**Compliance:** CAN-SPAM if commercial; GDPR if EU subscribers

**Voice:** Personal, voice-forward. Reads like a letter to a friend (or peer, depending on audience). First-person dominant.

## Newsletter Feature (3rd Party)

**Purpose:** Guest feature in someone else's newsletter — borrowed audience, authority loan.

**Word count:** Per host's spec (typically 800-1500 words)

**Structure:** Per host's spec — many newsletters have a template. Match it.

**Schema:** Typically not our control

**Distribution:**
- Host publishes
- We share to our audience as "featured in [Host]"
- Author bio in host's piece links back to us
- Cross-post on our domain after exclusivity period

**Compliance:**
- Host's editorial guidelines
- Host's exclusivity window (typically 30-90 days)
- Bio + link spec per host

**Voice:** Match host's voice. Subordinate our usual voice to fit their audience.

## App Store Listing (Apple)

**Purpose:** Convert App Store visitors to installs.

**Word count:**
- App Name: ≤30 chars
- Subtitle: ≤30 chars
- Description: ≤4000 chars (first ≤170 most-shown without "more" tap)
- Keywords field: ≤100 chars (comma-separated, no spaces, internal use only — NOT shown)
- What's New: ≤4000 chars

**Structure:**
1. **App Name** — primary keyword + brand
2. **Subtitle** — value prop in ≤30 chars
3. **Description first paragraph (most-shown)** — hook + key value (≤170 chars effective)
4. **Description body** — feature list, use cases, social proof, screenshots/video reference
5. **Keywords field** — research-driven, comma-separated, prioritized
6. **What's New** — release notes for current version

**Schema:** SoftwareApplication (typically Apple-handled)

**Distribution:**
- Apple App Store (primary)
- App Store Optimization (ASO) tools tracking
- Search Ads campaign (paid)
- Cross-platform website + landing page

**Compliance:**
- **Apple App Store Review Guidelines** (no competitor mentions, no promotional pricing in keywords, accurate screenshots)
- **Privacy nutrition label** accuracy
- **Localization** if shipping multi-region

**Voice:** Confident, value-clear, scannable. Avoid jargon. Lead with outcomes.

---

## App Store Listing (Google Play)

**Word count:**
- App Title: ≤30 chars
- Short Description: ≤80 chars (most-shown)
- Long Description: ≤4000 chars
- What's New: ≤500 chars

**Structure:** Similar to Apple but Short Description is the primary "above-fold" hook (≤80 chars, more visible than Apple's subtitle).

**Schema:** Google-handled

**Compliance:** Google Play Policy Center, especially around restricted content categories.

## G2 / Capterra / TrustRadius Listing

**Purpose:** Convert review-platform visitors to demo/trial.

**Word count:** Platform-specific (typically: headline + 200-400 word description + bulleted feature list)

**Structure:**
1. **Headline / value prop** — top of profile
2. **Description** — what we do, who for, why
3. **Feature list** — bulleted, scannable
4. **Use case section** — by industry / role / team size
5. **Pricing summary** (if requested by platform)
6. **Integrations list**

**Schema:** Platform-handled

**Distribution:**
- Platform profile is the primary distribution
- Reviews drive ranking (separate review-acquisition strategy)
- Profile updates push to "recently updated" feeds

**Compliance:**
- Platform vendor guidelines
- Review-aggregation rules (no incentivizing reviews per platform terms)
- Accuracy of pricing/features (claim disputes hurt ranking)

**Voice:** Buyer-language-heavy (per the platform's audience), use-case-anchored, scannable.
